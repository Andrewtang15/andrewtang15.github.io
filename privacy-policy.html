Privacy Policy

App: Hermes Assistant

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This is a personal, self-hosted assistant application used by a single account owner. It accesses Google Workspace data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets, Docs, Tasks) solely to provide personal productivity features such as a daily briefing and calendar lookups.

What data is accessed

  • Calendar events (read) — to show upcoming events in a daily briefing.
  • Gmail (read/send) — to summarize and, when explicitly requested, send email.
  • Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, Tasks — read and, where relevant, write, only on the owner's instruction.

How data is handled

  • All data is accessed only for the single authorized account owner.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own private server and are never transmitted to third parties.
  • Data is not sold, shared, or distributed to any third party.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time via their Google Account security settings.

Data retention

The application does not independently retain Google data beyond what is needed to operate. Removing the application or revoking access deletes the stored tokens and stops all access.

Contact

For any privacy questions, contact the app owner directly.

Personal self-hosted application. Not affiliated with Google LLC.
